Request Account & Data Deletion

Under Google Play Developer Policies and applicable data protection regulations (such as GDPR and CCPA), you have the right to request the permanent deletion of your TOPA operative account, personal data, and associated telemetry.

What Data Is Deleted?

  • Authentication Credentials: Your Firebase Authentication record and email identifier.
  • Operative Profile: Callsign, unique handle (e.g., @ghost_runner), age/birthdate record, and avatar URL.
  • Device Binding: The pseudonymous device token linking your profile to our game servers.
  • Note on Location Data: Because real-time GPS location data is processed strictly in-memory during active matches and discarded upon match completion, there are no persistent location tracks stored in our systems.

How to Submit a Deletion Request

You can request complete account deletion using either of the following methods:

  1. Email Request (Recommended): Send an email from your registered email address to:
    EMAIL DATA DELETION REQUEST
  2. Direct Support Dispatch: If you registered with a manual handle without an email address, email abziloo.support@gmail.com providing your unique handle (e.g., @handle) and operative callsign.

Processing Timeline

Upon receiving your request, our engineering team will verify operative ownership and permanently delete all personal data and credentials from active databases and backups within 30 calendar days. You will receive a confirmation email once the process is complete.

In-App Permission Revocation

If you wish to stop sharing location without deleting your operative account, you can revoke location permission directly on your device:
Settings ➔ Apps ➔ TOPA ➔ Permissions ➔ Location ➔ Don't allow.
