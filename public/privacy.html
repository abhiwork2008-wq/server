3. In-Depth Location Policy (Google Play Compliance)

Notice on Sensitive Location Data: TOPA accesses precise GPS coordinates strictly because physical spatial proximity is the fundamental mechanic of the game (verifying if a Hunter has sprinted within 5 meters of a Runner to confirm a tag).

A. Foreground Use Only

Location services are engaged only while the app is in the foreground during an active game session or during initial zone calibration by the match host. TOPA does NOT access or monitor background location when the app is minimized, closed, or when you are not in an active match.

B. Ephemeral RAM-Only Location Processing

GPS telemetry transmitted by your device is processed by our authoritative game engine strictly in volatile memory (RAM). Coordinates are evaluated in real-time to compute distance matrices and geofence intersections. We do not save persistent historical GPS tracks to persistent databases, and we do not compile location heatmaps or movement profiles.

C. Real-Time In-Session Sharing

During an active match, your calculated position is broadcast over encrypted WebSockets exclusively to other authorized players joined into the exact same private sector room (e.g., Hunters see radar pings; Runners see teammates). Your coordinates are never broadcast to the general public or unauthenticated parties.

4. Data We Do NOT Collect

To eliminate ambiguity, TOPA explicitly does NOT collect, access, or process:

  • Your phone contacts, address book, or SMS messages.
  • Your private camera feed or photo gallery (except when you explicitly choose to export/share a post-game tactical score card via the Android system share sheet).
  • Hardware identifiers such as IMEI, MAC Address, or Android Advertising ID (GAID / AAID).
  • Financial, payment, or biometric data.

5. Third-Party Services & Data Sharing Disclosures

TOPA does not sell, lease, or monetize your personal or location data to any third-party advertisers, data aggregators, or marketing brokers. We utilize only industry-standard cloud and map infrastructure to deliver the multiplayer service:

  • Google Play Services & Firebase (Google LLC):
    Used for secure user authentication (Firebase Authentication) and app distribution. Google's privacy practices are governed by the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Render Cloud Hosting (Render Services, Inc.):
    Hosts our authoritative multiplayer game server with TLS 1.3 encryption. Telemetry packets pass through secure cloud instances located in standard data centers.
  • OpenStreetMap Foundation:
    The in-game map view displays geographic map tiles downloaded from OpenStreetMap public tile servers. No user GPS coordinates, operative handles, or device IDs are transmitted to OpenStreetMap.

6. Data Security Practices

We enforce strict technical safeguards to protect your telemetry and account data:

  • Transport Layer Security: All client-server communications use HTTPS and TLS 1.3 / WSS (WebSocket Secure) encryption to prevent packet interception or man-in-the-middle attacks.
  • Isolated Room Scope: Match data is compartmentalized by unique cryptographic room codes; players in one sector cannot intercept data from another sector.
  • Zero Long-Term Storage of Coordinates: Once a match is finalized, room records and all associated location points in server memory are automatically destroyed.

7. Children's Privacy (13+ Requirement)

Age Gate (13+): TOPA is strictly designed and rated for players aged 13 and older. We do not intentionally solicit, collect, or maintain information from children under the age of 13.

During onboarding, operative registration includes an age-verification gate requiring players to confirm they are 13 or older. If you believe that a child under 13 has provided personal data to TOPA, please notify us immediately at abziloo.support@gmail.com, and we will promptly delete all associated records.

8. Your Rights, Permission Revocation & Data Deletion

A. Revoking Location Permissions

You can revoke GPS location permission at any time through your Android operating system settings:
Settings ➔ Apps ➔ TOPA ➔ Permissions ➔ Location ➔ Don't allow.
Note: Revoking location permissions will prevent participation in active matches since physical tag proximity cannot be verified.

B. Account & Data Deletion Request (Right to Erasure)

In accordance with Google Play's Account Deletion Policy and global privacy regulations (GDPR / CCPA), users have the right to request the permanent deletion of their account and all associated profile data.

To request deletion of your operative account and data:

  1. Submit a deletion request via our dedicated online portal at: https://tapapo-server.onrender.com/data-deletion.
  2. Or email us at abziloo.support@gmail.com with your operative callsign or registered email.

All verified account deletion requests are fully executed within 30 days, removing all authentication records, handles, and profile data from our databases.
